We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something isn’t right, here’s how we handle returns.  

## Eligibility for Returns

- You can return most items within 30 days of receiving them.  
- Items must be in the same condition you received them — including any accessories, manuals, or packaging where applicable.  
- For refurbished or used items, the condition will be as described in the listing. Please check carefully before purchase.  
- Certain items cannot be returned unless faulty:
  - Software, codes, or digital downloads.  
  - Items sold as “spares or repairs.”  

## Faulty or Damaged Items

- If your item arrives faulty or damaged, contact us within 48 hours with your order number and clear photos of the problem.  
- We’ll arrange either:
  - A replacement (if available)  
  - A repair  
  - Or a full refund.  

## Change of Mind

- You can return items if you change your mind within 30 days.  
- You’ll be responsible for return shipping costs.  
- The item must arrive back in the same condition it was sent.  

## Refunds

- Refunds are processed within 7 business days of receiving and inspecting the returned item.  
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method only.  

## How to Return an Item

1. Contact us at hello@controlaltrepeat.net with your order number and reason for return.  
2. We’ll give you a return reference and instructions.  
3. Pack the item securely to avoid damage in transit.  
4. Send it back using a tracked service.  

## International Returns

- If you’re outside the UK, you’ll be responsible for any return shipping costs, customs forms, and duties (unless the item is faulty).
